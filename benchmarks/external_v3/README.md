# External V3 expansion (frozen source, new cases)
18 additional files, nine upstream implementation families with matched synthetic single-edit faults. These are externally authored base algorithms, **not 18 independently authored real student solutions**. V2 pilot had 12 files, including three historical faults. In total the two cohorts contain 30 adapted files (15 controls, three historical faults, 12 synthetic mutants). They are separate cohorts on different engine revisions and must not be pooled as a single measured rate.
V3 engine was fixed at 09e3920 before this corpus was committed; no edits to the engine based on these outcomes. Some implementations share algorithmic ancestry (especially Kadane), and one upstream repository is the source; no claim of complete independence or model-training blindness. Archived sources are text copies with LF line endings. Exact wrappers, mutations, known preflight inputs and MIT notices are preserved. All labels require bounded preflight and independent human review. Discovery never receives the preflight failing input. If any preflight fails, preserve the failure, investigate the source/adapter, and do not silently discard the program.

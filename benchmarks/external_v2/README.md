# External-source pilot, post-V2 freeze
Twelve files in six paired implementation families: six upstream-intended controls, three historical faulty implementations and three deliberate single-edit mutants. Sources are independent of AlgoX-Ray development, but paired files and families overlap in algorithmic concepts; mutants are not authentic student failures. The older recursive and exponential implementations share search ancestry. No claim of blind human annotation, independence from model training, or 12 independent authors. Source archived at pinned commits; wrappers and mutations explicit. MIT notice retained. Known cases are label checks, not supplied to discovery. All files must pass an independent bounded label preflight before measurement. Engine remains at the already measured V2 source; no diagnostic tuning on this cohort. This is a pilot, not the originally proposed 30 externally authored solutions.
